## 10. Important Notes

1. **Legal Review Required**
   - Before going live, all pricing, terms, and policies should be reviewed by local counsel.
   - Cambodia tax and employment law compliance should be verified.
   - International payment and export rules should be confirmed.

2. **Pricing Adjustment**
   - Prices can be adjusted based on market feedback and operating costs.
   - Grandfather existing customers for 6-12 months before price increases.
   - Communicate price changes 30+ days in advance.

3. **Support and SLA**
   - Publish realistic SLAs that match your operational capacity.
   - Start conservative (best effort) and upgrade as team grows.
   - Document escalation process clearly.

4. **Data Protection**
   - Ensure all user data is handled per Privacy Policy.
   - Implement deletion and export processes as documented.
   - Maintain audit logs for compliance verification.

5. **Growth Planning**
   - Scale infrastructure based on usage growth.
   - Plan for tax/accounting as revenue increases.
   - Consider additional team members for support and operations.
