# Cambodia 90-Day Launch Plan

Version 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-10-02

This plan is designed for the Cambodia market and focuses on a low-cost, local-friendly launch strategy, fast onboarding, and steady adoption.

---

## Objective

Launch the application successfully in Cambodia with a local-friendly approach, low entry pricing, and strong customer support. The goal is to acquire early users, validate demand, fix adoption issues, and build trust before expanding to larger or international markets.

---

## Phase 1: Preparation and Setup (Days 1-15)

### Goals
- Finalize product readiness
- Prepare local sales and onboarding materials
- Confirm support and billing setup
- Validate the Cambodia launch experience

### Tasks

#### Product and Technical Readiness
- Confirm the app is stable for beta users
- Test key workflows end-to-end
- Confirm login, billing, support contact, and account access
- Verify security, monitoring, and backups
- Test data export and account deletion flows
- Prepare a basic demo environment or onboarding walkthrough

#### Legal and Commercial Readiness
- Publish Privacy Policy and EULA
- Confirm pricing and billing process
- Confirm local payment methods and invoice flow
- Prepare simple user onboarding instructions
- Finalize support contact channels

#### Sales and Marketing Preparation
- Prepare a Cambodia landing page or pricing page
- Create a short overview of product value for local users
- Prepare simple onboarding scripts for sales/support teams
- Create FAQ for common questions

### Deliverables
- Functional app ready for pilot users
- Local pricing page live
- Contact, support, and billing flow tested
- Simple onboarding materials ready

---

## Phase 2: Local Pilot and Early Validation (Days 16-30)

### Goals
- Validate demand in Cambodia
- Test local pricing and messaging
- Fix core onboarding and support issues
- Gather early user feedback

### Tasks

#### Pilot User Recruitment
- Target employees, internal teams, and small businesses in Cambodia
- Start with 10-20 pilot users or small groups
- Offer a discounted or free pilot for early supporters
- Focus on teams that have real use cases and urgency

#### Pilot Experience
- Guide users through onboarding
- Track activation rate
- Measure time to first value
- Capture feedback on usability and support
- Review billing friction and renewal expectations

#### Support and Improvement
- Monitor issues and performance
- Improve onboarding steps based on real usage
- Fix major bugs before scaling
- Simplify user instructions where confusion exists

### Key Metrics
- User activation rate
- Number of pilot signups
- Time to first successful use
- Number of support requests
- Conversion from trial to paid plan

### Deliverables
- Feedback summary from pilot users
- Updated onboarding flow
- Early product and pricing improvements
- Launch-ready support process

---

## Phase 3: Soft Cambodia Launch (Days 31-60)

### Goals
- Open the Cambodia market with limited scale
- Start selling at low local pricing
- Support early conversion and usage
- Build trust, testimonials, and references

### Tasks

#### Launch Offer
- Launch Cambodia Starter plan at low price
- Offer annual discount or special local onboarding offer
- Promote to internal teams, SMEs, and local partners
- Publish a simple value proposition tailored to Cambodia

#### Sales and Outreach
- Contact local potential customers directly
- Offer short onboarding calls
- Build referral relationships with local partners
- Use WhatsApp, email, and local communication channels

#### Customer Support Setup
- Set up a basic support helpdesk or email response flow
- Maintain a response time target of 24-48 hours in early phases
- Log issues and feature requests
- Offer simple training to new customers

### Marketing Focus
- Highlight affordability, simplicity, and trust
- Emphasize local support and on-boarding assistance
- Position the app as practical and business-ready

### Key Metrics
- New customers acquired
- Conversion from trial to paid plan
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Support response time
- User retention after 30 days

### Deliverables
- Cambodia launch active
- First paying customers live
- Support and onboarding process documented
- Customer feedback loop active

---

## Phase 4: Post-Launch Optimization (Days 61-90)

### Goals
- Improve retention and sustainable growth
- Optimize local pricing and support
- Prepare for global expansion or larger enterprise sales

### Tasks

#### Monitor Performance
- Review adoption and retention trends
- Identify users who may need more support
- Review churn and payment issues
- Identify best-performing plans and customer types

#### Improve Offer
- Adjust pricing if conversion is weak or too strong
- Add a mid-tier plan if needed
- Improve onboarding based on user behavior
- Create simple reference cases or success stories

#### Build Local Reputation
- Gather testimonials from early customers
- Track and document customer wins
- Collect case studies and example use cases
- Prepare their stories for future sales

#### Prepare for Global Expansion
- Review which features are most requested by customers
- Identify enterprise-ready features
- Prepare plans for English-language support and bigger accounts

### Deliverables
- Refined local pricing and support model
- Improved onboarding and retention strategy
- Market validation report
- Expansion readiness checklist

## Suggested Customer Targets

### Target 1: Internal Teams
- Internal operations teams
- Admin or business support teams
- Local departments with simple workflows

### Target 2: SMEs
- Small and medium businesses
- Teams with repeated daily operations
- Businesses wanting affordable workflow tools

### Target 3: Partners and Local Organizations
- Local resellers or business partners
- NGOs or community organizations
- Business supporters operating in Cambodia

---

## KPIs for the 90-Day Launch

- Number of signups
- Number of paying customers
- Activation rate
- Conversion from free trial to paid plan
- Retention after 30 days
- Number of support tickets
- Average time to value
- Monthly recurring revenue

---

## Risk Controls

- Keep pricing affordable to avoid customer hesitation
- Keep support simple and responsive
- Avoid overbuilding before market validation
- Avoid too many custom features before demand is proven
- Focus on reliability and ease of use first

---

## Final Recommendation

The Cambodia launch should stay focused, local, and affordable. The first 90 days should prioritize trust, activation, and stable growth rather than broad expansion. Once early customers are using the product successfully and support processes are reliable, expansion into larger or international markets becomes much easier.
