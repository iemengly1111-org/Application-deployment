# Launch QA Checklist

This checklist is used to review the landing page and launch materials before public release.

## 1. Headline and Value Proposition
- [ ] The headline clearly explains what the product is
- [ ] The subheadline explains the main value in plain language
- [ ] Users can understand the offer in under 5 seconds
- [ ] The page clearly communicates the Cambodia market focus

## 2. CTA and Conversion
- [ ] “Get Started” button is clear and visible
- [ ] “Book a Demo” button is present and useful
- [ ] Contact CTA is visible near pricing and footer
- [ ] All CTA buttons lead to the correct destination
- [ ] Email and contact flow are working

## 3. Product and Mockup Review
- [ ] The dashboard mockup looks realistic and polished
- [ ] Product messaging matches the mockup
- [ ] The product section helps users understand the app
- [ ] The section looks clean and not crowded

## 4. Pricing Review
- [ ] Starter pricing is clear and realistic
- [ ] Professional pricing is understandable
- [ ] Enterprise pricing is clearly separate
- [ ] Pricing fits the Cambodia market strategy
- [ ] Trial or demo offer is visible

## 5. Trust and Credibility
- [ ] Privacy Policy exists and is linked
- [ ] EULA exists and is linked
- [ ] License exists and is linked
- [ ] Support and contact details are visible
- [ ] The page feels professional and trustworthy

## 6. Mobile and Responsive Design
- [ ] The page works well on mobile
- [ ] Headings are readable on mobile
- [ ] Buttons are easy to tap
- [ ] Links and sections stack correctly
- [ ] Layout remains clean at smaller widths

## 7. Visual Quality
- [ ] Fonts are readable
- [ ] Spacing is consistent
- [ ] Buttons and cards are aligned
- [ ] Colors are cohesive and accessible
- [ ] The page looks polished and not unfinished

## 8. Functionality and Links
- [ ] All navigation links work
- [ ] Footer links work
- [ ] Email links are valid
- [ ] No broken buttons or placeholders remain
- [ ] The page has no obvious rendering issues

## 9. Local Market Fit
- [ ] Pricing feels realistic for Cambodia
- [ ] Messaging fits Cambodian business needs
- [ ] The tone is simple, clear, and professional
- [ ] Support and onboarding language fits the local market

## 10. Final Launch Readiness
- [ ] Page content is consistent with the product
- [ ] The contact email is correct
- [ ] Legal and support pages are available
- [ ] The landing page is ready for public launch
- [ ] A human reviewer has read the page once and approved it

## Recommended Launch Decision
- [ ] Ready to publish
- [ ] Needs minor edits
- [ ] Needs major revision before launch
